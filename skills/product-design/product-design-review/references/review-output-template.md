# Review Output Template

## Executive summary
- Product/context (1-2 sentences)
- Top 3 issues by impact
- Quick wins (1-2 items)
- Strategic opportunities (1-2 items)

## Findings (prioritized)
Use a compact table if possible.

Columns:
- Issue
- Evidence/location
- Impact
- Severity (0-3)
- Recommendation
- Effort (S/M/L)
- Confidence (Low/Med/High)

## Detailed findings by flow or task
- Flow/task name
- Observations
- Friction points
- Recommendations

## Accessibility notes
- Blockers
- Fixes and owners

## Experiments or validation
- Hypothesis
- Proposed test
- Success metric

## Assumptions and open questions
- List missing inputs or constraints

## Next steps
- Immediate actions (1-3)
- Follow-up research or tracking
